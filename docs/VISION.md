# Product Vision

## What Ampliforge Is

An enterprise-grade content-repurposing engine: one long-form video → a **platform-optimized, brand-consistent week of content** (copy + visuals) in under two minutes of machine time and ~30–45 minutes of human review.

Not a summarizer. It extracts insights, rewrites for each platform’s expectations, and packages outputs with professional design — acting as creative director, copywriter, strategist, and designer in concert.

---

## Problem

Fragmented audiences require different narrative structures, visuals, hooks, pacing, CTAs, and posting strategies. Manual repurposing costs **8–15 hours per video**. Creators burn out, ship weak adaptations, or skip distribution.

Ampliforge collapses the heavy lift; humans personalize, approve, and engage.

---

## Philosophy: Multiplier, Not Replacer

| Platform | Creator |
|----------|---------|
| Structure, strategy, visuals, speed | Personality, context, connection, approval |

Every output is a professional starting point — never a substitute for the creator’s voice.

---

## Design constraints (binding)

These override feature sprawl from the long-form vision paste:

1. **Depth over volume** — Curated kit first; full enterprise package later.
2. **Review studio first** — Edit / preview / accept / regenerate is the hero, not a cluttered command center.
3. **Platform-native craft** — LinkedIn ≠ X ≠ IG ≠ email.
4. **Brand early, distribute late** — Identity before calendar and publish APIs.
5. **Templates before generative art** — Satori/Resvg default; AI images optional/labeled.
6. **Section-level control** — Contextual regenerate preserves edits.
7. **Prove value with abuse guards** — Guest preview only with hard rate/cost caps.
8. **Token-native UI** — Build on the enterprise design system (DTCG → CSS vars). No raw hex. Dark-first with light fallback.
9. **Don’t break the pipeline casually** — `lib/pipeline/process-project.ts` is real-time UX critical; extend steps carefully.

---

## Success (near-term)

A signed-in creator can paste a URL, receive a curated kit with brand-consistent visuals, review in one focused UI, and export — without a full creative team.

## Explicit non-goals (until later phases)

- Full Buffer/Hootsuite replacement
- Every format on every platform on day one
- 60+ visual templates before review quality is excellent
- Live multi-network analytics before export/scheduling is loved
